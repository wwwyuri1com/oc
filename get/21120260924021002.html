Introduction

Actor Icy is the foundation of the Icy META. Within the Y1 OC universe, she appears as an actress across multiple IP series, including Icy & Arte, DDD Studio, NO1 Office, and more. The standalone series OC's Off Script Time also offers glimpses into her personal life from a hypothetical actor's perspective. She lives with Arte, and the two are unquestionably lovers. However, since getting to know NO6, could something subtle be starting to change?
