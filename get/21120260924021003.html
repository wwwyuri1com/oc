Introduction

Uranus Icy is the female protagonist of the IP series NO1 Office. She refers to NO6 as the “First Generation” and shows a strong attachment to her. Compared to other versions of the Icy META, her personality is a specialized blend of L.Icy's assertiveness and S.Icy's gentle softness. Within the IP storyline, she is the first character to attain NO1-level. Although she often displays a willful personality that can be difficult to handle, she has also shown her gentle and thoughtful side on numerous occasions. In the fictional setting, Uranus Icy is portrayed by Actor Icy.
